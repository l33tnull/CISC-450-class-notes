- consists of individuals and variables that give info about those individuals
- individual can be object or person
- variable is an attribute; measurement or label


## Types of Variables
### Categorical

-  take category or label values and place individual in one of several groups
- each observation can be placed in only one category
- categories are mutually exclusive
	- Ex:
		- smoker
		- nonsmoker
		- gender
		- race
### Quantitative

- take numerical values and represent some kind of measurement 
	- Ex: 
		- age
		- weight
		- height
